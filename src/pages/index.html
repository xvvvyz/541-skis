The ski we
couldn't find.

Jesse Scroggins grew up ski racing and spent years looking for one all‑mountain ski that could do it all. He never found it, so he and his friend Brad Duffy, a former racer and board sports engineer, started 541 from Brad's original designs. For more than a decade, every shape has been tested and refined on Mt. Hood, where you can ski year‑round. If we don't like it, we don't sell it.
